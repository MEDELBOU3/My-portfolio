I’m Mohamed El Bouanani, a computer science and artificial intelligence student from Morocco.

I enjoy creating things from the ground up and understanding how complex systems work internally. My interests move between game-engine architecture, real-time graphics, animation, web applications, algorithms and interface design.

Outside the code, I’m drawn to photography and graphic design — and to the visual language of architecture, machines, landscapes and cinema. I’m also fascinated by Formula 1 engineering and would love to explore Japan one day.
